# Tempo

How fast or slow the song moves. Measured in BPM (beats per minute).

**Think of it as:** The song's speedometer.

**Examples:**

- 60 BPM = one beat per second (like a clock ticking)
- 120 BPM = twice as fast
- Your resting heartbeat ≈ 60-80 BPM

**See:** [Beats & Rhythms](../../beats-and-rhythms.md) for full BPM breakdown and production technicalities (including the trap tempo paradox).

**In AI prompts:**

- "Slow tempo" or "60 BPM" see [Slow Tempo Examples](/samples/tempo/slow-tempo.md)
- "Uptempo" or "130 BPM" see [Uptempo Examples](/samples/tempo/uptempo.md)
- "Moderate pace" see [Moderate Pace Examples](/samples/tempo/moderate-pace.md)

**Important:** Some genres have tempo quirks where perceived speed differs from actual BPM. See [Production Technicalities](../../beats-and-rhythms.md#production-technicalities-what-you-need-to-know) for details on trap (70 BPM but feels like 140), dubstep (140 BPM but feels like 70), and other tempo confusions.
