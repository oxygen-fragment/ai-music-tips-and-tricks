# Rhythm

When notes happen. The timing pattern.

**Think of it as:** The difference between speech (free-flowing) and a drumbeat (structured timing).

**Examples:**

- Clapping along to a song = following rhythm
- Drum patterns
- When syllables hit in lyrics

**Not the same as:** Tempo (which is speed). Rhythm is the pattern, tempo is how fast you play the pattern.
