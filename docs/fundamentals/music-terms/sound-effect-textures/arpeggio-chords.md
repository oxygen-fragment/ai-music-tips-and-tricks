# Arpeggio Chords

Notes of a chord played one at a time instead of all together.

**Think of it like:** Climbing stairs one step at a time instead of jumping to the top. Same destination (the chord), different journey.

**Examples:**

- Classical guitar pieces (finger-picking patterns)
- Piano intro to "Clocks" by Coldplay
- Harp sounds (almost always arpeggios)
- "Stairway to Heaven" intro

**How it sounds:** Flowing, elegant, less aggressive than strumming full chords. Creates movement and texture.

**Why use it:**

- Adds sophistication without changing the harmony
- Creates rhythmic interest (each note is a separate event)
- Works well for intros, backgrounds, or ambient sections
- Softer than full chords but more active than single melody

**In songs:** Often used in intros, verses (under vocals), or as connecting sections between parts.
