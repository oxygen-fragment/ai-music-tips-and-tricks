# Staccato

**What it is:** Short, detached, choppy notes.

**Real-world equivalent:**

- Speaking in. Short. Bursts. (staccato)
- vs speaking smoothly in connected phrases (legato)

**Why use it:** Creates tension, urgency, or playfulness.

**In AI prompts:**

- "Staccato strings"
- "Choppy rhythm"
- "Short, punchy notes"
