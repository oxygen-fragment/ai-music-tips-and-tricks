# Distortion

**What it is:** Intentional audio "fuzziness" or "grittiness."

**Think of it as:** Taking a clean sound and roughing it up.

**Examples:**

- Electric guitar overdrive
- Screaming/growling vocals
- Lo-fi/crackly aesthetic

**Why use it:** Adds aggression, energy, or vintage character.

**In AI prompts:**

- "Distorted guitar"
- "Clean tone" (opposite)
- "Gritty vocals"
