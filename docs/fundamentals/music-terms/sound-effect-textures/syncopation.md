# Syncopation

**What it is:** Emphasizing the off-beats instead of main beats.

**Think of it as:** Musical surprise. Notes hitting where you don't expect them.

**Examples:**

- Reggae's choppy guitar (hits between drum beats)
- Funk bass lines
- Makes you want to move in unexpected ways

**Why use it:** Creates groove, interest, prevents predictability.

**In AI prompts:**

- "Syncopated rhythm"
- "Off-beat emphasis"
- "Funky groove"
