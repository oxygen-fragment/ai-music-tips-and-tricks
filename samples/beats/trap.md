# Trap Beats

Trap beats are characterized by **sparse kick/snare patterns** at 70-80 BPM combined with **fast, rolling hi-hats** that create a double-time feel (140-160 BPM perception).

**Key production technicality:** Trap is produced at 70-80 BPM in the DAW, but the hi-hats are programmed at double-time, making it *feel* much faster.

---

## The Trap Tempo Paradox

### 70 BPM (Actual) vs 140 BPM (Perceived)

**Producer perspective:**

- Project tempo: 70-80 BPM
- Kick and snare hit on the quarter notes (slow)
- Hi-hats programmed as 16th or 32nd notes (double/triple speed)

**Listener perspective:**

- The fast hi-hats dominate the rhythm
- Creates the perception of 140-160 BPM
- Even though the kick is slow, it feels energetic

**Why this matters for AI:**

- If you prompt "140 BPM trap," AI might make the kick too fast
- Proper prompting: "70 BPM trap with rolling hi-hats"

## Sample Prompts & Explanations

### Standard Trap Beat (70 BPM)

**Prompt:**

```
70 BPM trap beat with rolling hi-hats, heavy 808 bass, and minimal kick pattern
```

**What to expect:**

- Slow, punchy kick pattern
- Fast, intricate hi-hat rolls
- Deep, sustained 808 bass hits
- Sparse snare on 2 and 4 (or variation)

**Why this works:**

- Specifies actual tempo (70 BPM)
- Mentions "rolling hi-hats" to ensure double-time feel
- Highlights 808 bass (signature trap element)

---

### Dark, Aggressive Trap (75 BPM)

**Prompt:**

```
75 BPM dark trap beat with triplet hi-hats, distorted 808s, and ominous synth pads
```

**What to expect:**

- Menacing atmosphere
- Complex hi-hat patterns (triplet rolls)
- Gritty, overdriven 808 bass
- Dark ambient textures

**Why this works:**

- "Dark" sets the mood
- "Triplet hi-hats" adds rhythmic complexity
- "Distorted 808s" clarifies the bass timbre

---

### Melodic Trap (80 BPM)

**Prompt:**

```
80 BPM melodic trap with pitched 808 slides, atmospheric pads, and arpeggiated synth melody
```

**What to expect:**

- Tuned 808 basslines that slide between notes
- Lush, ambient background
- Melodic lead elements

**Why this works:**

- "Melodic" indicates musical vs purely rhythmic focus
- "Pitched 808 slides" is a trap signature technique
- "Arpeggiated synth" adds movement

---

### Drill Sub-Genre (60 BPM)

**Prompt:**

```
60 BPM drill beat with sliding 808s, aggressive hi-hat rolls, and dark piano melody
```

**What to expect:**

- Even slower tempo than standard trap
- Sinister, menacing vibe
- UK/Chicago drill aesthetic

**Why this works:**

- Drill is a slower trap variant (60-70 BPM)
- "Sliding 808s" and "dark piano" are drill staples

## Common Mistakes When Prompting for Trap

### L Mistake 1: Confusing Perceived Tempo with Actual Tempo

**Bad prompt:**

```
140 BPM trap beat
```

**Problem:**

- AI might interpret this as 140 BPM kick/snare (way too fast)
- Actual trap is 70-80 BPM with double-time hi-hats

**Fix:**

```
70 BPM trap beat with rolling hi-hats and 808 bass
```

---

### L Mistake 2: Not Specifying Hi-Hat Style

**Bad prompt:**

```
75 BPM trap
```

**Problem:**

- Doesn't specify hi-hat complexity
- Could result in basic, non-trap hi-hat pattern

**Fix:**

```
75 BPM trap with rolling hi-hats and triplet patterns
```

---

### L Mistake 3: Forgetting the 808 Bass

**Bad prompt:**

```
Trap beat with heavy drums
```

**Problem:**

- No mention of 808 bass (the heart of trap)
- No tempo specified

**Fix:**

```
70 BPM trap beat with booming 808 bass and sparse kick pattern
```

## Trap Beat Characteristics

### Tempo Range

- **Standard trap:** 70-80 BPM
- **Drill (trap sub-genre):** 60-70 BPM
- **Uptempo trap:** 80-90 BPM (less common)

### Drum Pattern Elements

**Kick:**

- Sparse, punchy
- Often on beat 1 and sometimes beat 3
- Less frequent than other genres

**Snare:**

- Usually on beat 3 (or 2 and 4 in some variants)
- Heavy, sometimes layered with claps
- Often has reverb/delay tail

**Hi-Hats:**

- The defining element of trap
- Rapid 16th or 32nd note patterns
- Frequent rolls and triplet patterns
- Often velocity-varied for groove

**808 Bass:**

- Deep, sustained bass hits
- Often tuned/pitched to follow melody
- Characteristic "slide" between notes
- Sometimes distorted for aggression

### Common Sound Palette

- Heavy 808 kick and bass
- Crisp, metallic hi-hats
- Snappy snares with reverb
- Dark, atmospheric synth pads
- Arpeggiated melodies (melodic trap)
- Pitched vocal chops (some variants)
